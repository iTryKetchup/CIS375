Career Tracker Help Guide

Dashboard | Add New Job | Help | STAR Interview Cheat Sheet

Career Tracker is designed to help users organize job applications in one place. It supports manual application entry, status tracking, follow-up visibility, job-specific notes, resume and cover letter version tracking, references, and interview preparation resources. The dashboard is meant for quick scanning, while the Job Details page holds deeper information for each application.

+ Add New Job
  • Use Add New Job to enter a new application manually.
  • Fill in company name, job title, source, date applied, status, and follow-up date.
  • Job posting URL, resume version, cover letter version, and application stage notes are optional but helpful.
  • A suggested follow-up date may be added automatically, but it can be changed manually.
  • After saving, the application appears on the dashboard.
View All Jobs / Dashboard
  • The dashboard is designed for quick scanning and workflow triage.
  • Use the sticky status bar to filter by All, Applied, Interview, Follow-Up, Offer, or Rejected.
  • Use Search from the sticky bar to find applications by company name or job title.
  • Past Due and Upcoming provide quick follow-up visibility.
  • Cards are collapsible so the dashboard stays clean.
  • Use View Details to open the full application record.
Job Details
  • Job Details is the command center for a specific application.
  • Use it to update job information, status, follow-up date, job posting URL, resume version, cover letter version, notes, and extended notes.
  • Application Stage Notes are for short notes from the application stage.
  • Extended Notes are for deeper research, interview prep, salary notes, STAR drafts, or detailed planning.
  • References Used lets the user add, edit, or delete references connected to that job.
  • Use Update Job Details to save changes.
STAR Interview Help
  • STAR stands for Situation, Task, Action, and Result.
  • Use the STAR Interview Cheat Sheet to prepare behavioral interview answers.
  • The STAR page includes sections for in-person, phone, and virtual interviews.
  • It also includes story bank ideas and practice drills.
  • Job-specific STAR drafts can be stored in Extended Notes on the Job Details page.
